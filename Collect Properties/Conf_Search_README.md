Conformer Searching and DFT Optimization Workflow
1.	Candidate monomers were encoded as SMILES strings and converted into SDF and XYZ using OpenBabel, then minimized with Universal Force Field (UUF). If errors arose due to the size of the molecule, RDKit was used, then minimized with either MMFF (Merck Molecular Force Field) or UFF. 
2.	3D geometries were subjected to conformational searches using Conformer-Rotamer Ensemble Sampling Tool (CREST, version 2.12, from Grimme and coworkers) with the GFN-FF (xTB version 6.4.0). An energy window of 5 kcal mol–1 and subsequent PCA/k-Means clustering were applied to refine the conformer ensemble to 50 or fewer conformers per structure, representing energetically relevant and conformationally diverse monomer flexibility.
3.	The conformer ensembles were subjected to DFT geometry optimization and energy correction calculations using Gaussian (Gaussian16, Revision C.01) at the ωB97X-D/def2-TZVP//ωB97X-D/def2-SVP level of theory in gas phase with standard temperature and pressure (298.15 K, 1 atm). The ωB97X-D functional includes empirical dispersion correction. Determination of the natural bond orbitals (NBO) was performed using the NBO program (version 7.0). NMR shifts were computed using the gauge-invariant atomic orbitals (GIAO) method. For conformers where the optimization calculation did not terminate normally or the frequency calculations resulted in an imaginary frequency more negative than -100 cm-1, the conformer was resubmitted specifying tight convergence criteria (using Gaussian keyword opt=tight) and the calculation of force constants (using Gaussian keyword opt=calcfc). Several attempts were made; however, if a job could not terminate normally or an optimized structure devoid of any negative frequencies could not be obtained for a given conformer, that conformer was removed from the ensemble for descriptor collection. An energy window cutoff of 5 kcal mol–1 was repeated prior to feature collection, using DFT-computed energies.
